# Community code of conduct

Civic Ledger welcomes good-faith collaboration across political views. Participate professionally, discuss evidence and implementation rather than personal motives, and do not harass, threaten, expose private information, or make unsupported accusations about public officials or contributors.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe, lawful, focused, and evidence-based. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
