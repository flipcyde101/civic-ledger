# Roadmap

## Verified ledger

- Permission-reviewed House and Senate collectors
- Immutable raw-document archive, OCR/table extraction, and review queue
- Amendment/correction reconciliation and public coverage dashboard
- Member, issuer, filing, and ticker detail pages

## Personal intelligence

- Server-synchronized accounts, saved views, watchlists, and alert delivery
- Email, push, webhook, CSV, and API exports
- Estimated position ranges incorporating annual filings and partial sales

## Context engine

- Time-aware committee membership, legislation, federal contracts, and lobbying context
- Versioned signal methodology, explanations, and neutral anomaly detection
- Licensed total-return series using only information available after public filing

## Research platform

- Documented public API, notebooks, team workspaces, and methodology audits
- Accessibility, localization, retention controls, and observability
- Reproducible benchmarks for coverage, correction rate, latency, and entity-resolution accuracy
